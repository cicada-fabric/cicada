### Uncertain issue/revoke outcomes

For a lost issue/revoke response while the exact RPC packet remains pending, all new RPCs wait while that original packet is recovered. A validated cached/recovered `COMPLETED` result for that pending packet can finish the operation and clear the pending context. If authenticated recovery instead returns terminal `OUTCOME_UNCERTAIN`, retain the original ciphertext/counters and leave the dedicated regroup fence blocked indefinitely. Under the current Core contract there is no later correlated `COMPLETED` result or automatic/manual clearing route after that terminal response. A generic `status.snapshot` or a proposal's current `delegation` projection cannot clear the fence or authorize a replacement write. Any future reconciliation requires a separately authorized Core contract. Do not retry under a new operation ID.
